about

PK Corporation

PK Corporation is an independent software company built around one idea: build useful software, keep it simple, make it accessible. PK stands for Phana Kurosaki, the person behind it.

The company focuses on developer tools, creative tools, AI-assisted products, and everyday applications — software meant to be used, not just demoed.

PK Khmer Type is the first product to reach a working, usable state, and it's the current focus. PK Icons Studio is available too, though it's still being built. Everything else on this site is in active development or planning, shown here honestly rather than dressed up.

The long-term direction is a small, coherent family of tools that share a design language and a philosophy, rather than a scattered pile of unrelated apps.
